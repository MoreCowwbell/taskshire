# Bundled art — the medieval village

The `.glb` files in this directory are built from three CC0 asset packs by
**[Kay Lousberg](https://kaylousberg.com)**, and the village also plants from one by
**[Kenney](https://kenney.nl)** that it borrows from the space theme's folder. They are **not**
covered by the project's MIT licence — they are
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), which places them in the public
domain. Each licence below is as read from that pack's own `License.txt`
on 2026-09-13; the Medieval Hexagon Pack's was read again on 2026-09-15 when `helmet` was
added and on 2026-09-16 for the neutral `ship` and the ruin's `building_stage_C` and
`resource_lumber`, and is the same CC0 text in its FREE, EXTRA and SOURCE tiers.

| File | Built from | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `medieval.glb` | [KayKit : Medieval Hexagon Pack](https://kaylousberg.itch.io/kaykit-medieval-hexagon-pack) 1.0 (SOURCE tier) | CC0 1.0 |
| `forest.glb` | [KayKit : Medieval Hexagon Pack](https://kaylousberg.itch.io/kaykit-medieval-hexagon-pack) 1.0 (SOURCE tier) | CC0 1.0 |
| `decay.glb` | [KayKit : Medieval Hexagon Pack](https://kaylousberg.itch.io/kaykit-medieval-hexagon-pack) 1.0 (SOURCE tier) | CC0 1.0 |
| `../space/nature.glb` | [Nature Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit) by **[Kenney](https://kenney.nl)** — the space folder's `nature.glb`, borrowed rather than copied: palms, cacti, pink, autumn and jungle trees, recoloured and vertex-baked by `tools/build-nature.mjs` | CC0 1.0 |
| `crew.glb` | [KayKit : Adventurers](https://kaylousberg.itch.io/kaykit-adventurers) 2.0 (SOURCE tier) for the eight bodies, their colourway sheets, the hand tools and the round shield; [KayKit : Character Animations](https://kaylousberg.itch.io/kaykit-character-animations) 1.1 for the rig and its clips; the Medieval Hexagon Pack (SOURCE tier) for `hammer`, `shovel` and the helpers' `helmet` | CC0 1.0 |

Besides Kenney's plants, one thing here is not Kay's: the torch in `medieval.glb` is generated by
`src/themes/medieval/torch.mjs` rather than packed, because the pack has no light source in
it. It is MIT along with the code.

Packed by `npm run assets medieval` from the raw packs under `ASSETS_SRC`; see `docs/kits.md`
for what each kit holds and why the three are separate.

CC0 requires nothing of you. Crediting Kay and Kenney costs nothing either.
